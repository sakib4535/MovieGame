BEHIND THE SCENES

A story match.
Just for fun.

Each game draws 15 varied questions from its own question bank, with shuffled choices. Your answers form an eight-trait profile. We compare it with editorial character profiles using equal-weight distance. The five closest traits explain your match. Affinity is a game score, not a probability or a psychological assessment.

Movie identities and reference links accompany every entry. Personality profiles and themes are our interpretations. “Source checked” means a reference was checked during catalog preparation; other entries still need editorial verification. Plot details can contain spoilers.

No account, name, email, or external AI service is needed. Your quiz progress stays in this browser tab. A submitted result and a hash of your answers are retained on the server for up to 30 days when scheduled cleanup is enabled. Rate limits use a short-lived hashed network address. Clear this tab’s progress with “Start over.” Sharing is optional. The share buttons create a public, expiring result page with the display name you choose, character, and trait bars. Your full survey answers are not included. Shared images may remain in social platforms’ caches after the page expires. Instagram uses the device share sheet or an image download; it does not post automatically.

Film and character names belong to their respective owners. MovieGame is an independent fan experience.
